About

I am Director of Technology Operations at Entratus AI, where I help take voice agents from demo to production. That work is mostly evaluation: writing the definition of done, building caller personas, running test calls and deciding what has to be fixed before an agent goes live.

Before AI, I spent 35 years in broadcast audio, so I listen to agents the way an audio engineer does: latency, interruptions, mispronounced names and the half-second pauses that make callers hang up. Earlier, as Director of Marketing and Technology at Insurance Care Direct, I co-designed a dialer platform and built AI compliance and appointment-setting tools.

AI certifications include OpenAI Technical Practitioner, Foundational Knowledge and Consultative Solutions Practitioner. See all. Projects on this site use public tools and fictional scenarios only, never employer or client data.
